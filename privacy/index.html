Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

platonicman.com (the "Site") is run by Gary Tallman ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the Site collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: hello@platonicman.com.

1. Orders (signed copies)

Signed sets are sold through Stripe Checkout. When you order, Stripe collects your name, email, shipping address, payment details and anything you type in the optional fields (a name to sign the book to, a gift note, and whether you want the weekly joke email). We receive everything except your full card number, which only Stripe sees. We use this information to sign, ship and support your order, and to send the receipt. Stripe's handling of your data is covered by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

2. Email

We only add you to the weekly joke email if you choose "Yes" at checkout or sign up on the Site. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can write to us and we'll remove you.

3. Analytics and advertising

The Site uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel. They set cookies and collect technical information such as pages viewed, buttons clicked (for example "Buy on Amazon"), game scores, device, browser and approximate location. We use this to understand which pages and ads work and to show our ads on Facebook and Instagram to people who visited the Site. Google and Meta process this data under their own policies.

  • Opt out of Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Control Meta ads: Facebook Ad Preferences
  • Industry opt-out: optout.aboutads.info
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

4. Amazon and other sites

"Buy on Amazon" buttons take you to Amazon.com. Purchases there are governed by Amazon's own privacy policy. The same applies to YouTube videos embedded on the Site and our social media pages.

5. What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the services needed to run the Site and fill orders: Stripe (payments), the shipping carrier (USPS), our email provider, Netlify (hosting), Google and Meta (as described above).

6. How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for shipping, customer support, taxes and accounting. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, except what we're legally required to keep.

7. Your rights

Email hello@platonicman.com to access, correct or delete your information, or to opt out of email. We'll reply within 30 days.

8. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
