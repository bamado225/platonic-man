Shipping & Returns

Effective November 26, 2026

Shipping

Signed sets ship to US addresses only. Shipping is a flat $3 per order. Gary signs and ships within 2 business days by USPS, and delivery takes 5–8 business days. For Christmas delivery, order signed copies by December 14.

Returns & refunds

You can return unopened, undamaged books within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Please email hello@platonicman.com first so we can help. Books signed to a specific name (personalized) can't be returned unless they arrive damaged.

Damaged or lost in transit

If your order arrives damaged or never shows up, email us within 14 days with a photo or your order details, and we'll send a replacement or refund you.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval.

Amazon purchases

Paperbacks bought on Amazon follow Amazon's return policy.
